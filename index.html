Case overview

Plaintiff Elena Rivera alleges that Northgate Logistics terminated her employment after she reported unpaid overtime, meal-break violations, and falsified route logs to HR. Defendant maintains termination was performance-based. The integrated exhibits below map chronology, witness statements, payroll records, and internal communications.
